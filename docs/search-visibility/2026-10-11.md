Route added:

- /guides/ontario-fish-stocking-records

Added a sourced guide for interpreting Ontario fish stocking records. An in-browser worksheet turns one source row into a cautious note while always retaining the warning that a dated release does not prove present-day abundance, catchability, legal access or current regulations.

The guide separates the recent recreational subset from the 1900 to 2012 historical dataset, explains key interpretation questions and links stocking research into the existing access and regulations workflow. It does not claim that stocked fish remain present.

Sources checked October 11, 2026:
