FishAI Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026.

FishAI is local-first: catch logs, saved spots, tackle, profile data, and small saved catch thumbnails are stored in your browser by default rather than in a FishAI user database.

Data sent when you use network features

Weather requests send selected coordinates to Open-Meteo. Place search and reverse geocoding send search text or coordinates to Nominatim/OpenStreetMap services. Interactive maps load tiles from CARTO/OpenStreetMap infrastructure. AI analysis sends the photo you explicitly submit, plus an optional reference-object hint, to a configured AI provider. Stripe processes checkout and billing information. Resend may process your email address to deliver a secure subscription recovery message. Vercel hosts the site/API and processes normal request metadata.

Authentication

Owner and subscriber authorization uses short-lived HttpOnly cookies. The owner password and Stripe customer IDs are not stored in browser localStorage as authorization credentials.

Your controls

You can remove local FishAI data by clearing this site's browser storage. You can export catches to CSV from the app. Billing data is managed through Stripe.

Important

Do not treat FishAI AI output as a legal fishing-regulation source. Consult the current official rules for your jurisdiction and waterbody.
